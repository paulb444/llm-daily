# LLM Industry Daily — November 20, 2026


## ⚡ What Matters Today

- **Anthropic closes its IPO at about $93.5 billion gross after full option exercise.** It sold 1,127,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00, about $91.6 billion of it to Anthropic; with the concurrent private placement, $119.5 billion total, comprising: $93.5 billion IPO (including selling stockholders' shares) and $26.0 billion placement ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close)). Shares opened at $111.50 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-anthropic-stock-begins-trading-in-record-81b-raise-with-15t-valuation-192410598.html)); Reuters calls it the largest stock-market debut on record ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-clinches-biggest-ever-ipo-with-81-billion-offering-2026-11-18/)). **Take:** The placement buyers (AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, TSMC Partners) are Anthropic's chip and cloud suppliers, tying compute supply to equity.
- **California AG subpoenas Nvidia over China chip exports and investor disclosures.** Attorney General Rob Bonta is examining whether Nvidia or Jensen Huang's actions let China obtain advanced chips or confidential information, and whether Nvidia misled investors; House committees say Huang will sit for transcribed interviews December 1 ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/california-ag-issues-nvidia-with-subpoena-as-part-of-probe-into-chinese-chip-exports/)). **Take:** Beyond Huang's November 12 resignation, state, congressional and shareholder actions now land together on interim CEO Colette Kress.
- **Meta reopens its 2030 net-zero target as its net carbon footprint rises 45 percent to 11.9 million mtCO2e.** Electricity use rose 35 percent to 24.8TWh, which Meta says it matched fully with clean energy; it blames interconnection, permitting and transmission lag and gave no new date ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/meta-to-reevaluate-net-zero-timeline-on-the-back-of-record-emissions/)). **Take:** If full clean-energy matching cannot hold the footprint down, other hyperscalers' targets face the same test.
- **Seven states' ratepayer advocates ask PJM to delay its December capacity auction.** They cite a Monitoring Analytics warning that the forecast includes canceled data centers, which could add $5 billion to $10 billion for PJM's 67 million consumers; the auction runs Dec. 9 to Dec. 15 and a delay needs FERC approval ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/ratepayer-advocates-pjm-market-monitor-capacity-auction/875165/)). **Take:** Phantom data center demand has become a ratepayer cost fight, so forecast quality now carries political risk.
- **Tencent's Q3 capex jumps 225 percent year on year to RMB 65.1bn ($9.77bn).** It drove negative free cash flow of RMB 69.1 billion, attributed to AI infrastructure; cloud services revenue growth accelerated to 35 percent from 21 percent ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/saudi-social-and-livestreaming-platform-hoolive-migrates-to-tencent-cloud/)). **Take:** Tencent now spends beyond its cash flow on AI; Alibaba's AI cloud growth the same quarter (see Data Center Infrastructure) suggests Chinese cloud demand is holding.

## 🔌 US Power Grid Capacity

- **NYISO** projects New York's first statewide resource deficiency, about 225 MW beginning 2033 and more than 1,850 MW by 2036; it expects 1,700 MW of data center demand among 2,700 MW of new large loads ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/new-york-is-short-electric-resources-beginning-2033-iso/875155/)).
- **PJM interconnection filing, follow-up:** it defines large computational loads as 50MW or more, lets states and utilities charge operators directly for transmission costs, and asks FERC for a 60-day comment period and a ruling within 120 days. PJM projects data center demand tripling to 29GW by 2030 ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/pjm-plan-could-cut-data-center-and-power-plant-grid-connection-times-to-12-months/)).
- **Wyoming Intertie**, Platte and Carbon Counties, Wyoming: DOE offers a $2.8 billion conditional loan for Grid United's 1.8-GW, about 106-mile HVDC line linking SPP and WECC. Grid United says the loan covers 80% of construction costs and saves about $500 million in interest; DOE says $39 billion in ratepayer savings; online 2032 or 2033 per a company fact sheet ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/doe-funded-transmission-projects-could-boost-east-west-transfer-capacity-40/875021/)). **Take:** It addresses the interregional bottleneck Meta cites, but arrives years after data center load.
- **Google and Quaise**, near Newberry Volcano, Oregon: Google agrees to buy 50 MW from Project Obsidian for The Dalles, possibly as early as 2030. A Quaise spokesperson says the deal is valued at more than $500 million; the two-stage buildout is expected to cost about $350 million; the source does not say whether one includes the other ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/google-signs-50-mw-superhot-geothermal-ppa-with-quaise-energy/875217/)).
- **Google and Ohio Power**, Columbus, Ohio: Google will fully fund an approximately $10.8 million advanced-conductor upgrade on 15.5 miles of lines, making 24MW contracted for its data center available two years earlier; needs PUCO and Ohio Power Siting Board approval ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-to-fund-108m-transmission-line-upgrade-for-ohio-power-in-columbus/)). **Take:** Paying directly for grid upgrades buys speed and offers regulators a model for charging large loads.
